**Location:** Barrio Paso de Piedra, Córdoba Sierras, Argentina (private gated community)
**Capacity:** Up to 4 guests
**Layout:** 2 bedrooms, 2 bathrooms
**Minimum stay:** 3 nights
**Website:** https://www.quintasierras.com

A secluded private villa with a pool in the Sierras de Córdoba. Panoramic
valley views, sunrises, and star-filled skies. Located in a secure gated
community with no visible neighbours and total privacy.

Two bedrooms, two bathrooms — one with an antique clawfoot tub, another with
an outdoor rain shower under the stars. A wood stove for cool evenings, a
covered quincho (outdoor dining/grill area) for long Argentine dinners, and a
pool perched above the valley.

All highway-connected: Carlos Paz 20 minutes, Córdoba city and airport 30
minutes, Alta Gracia and San Clemente 40 minutes.

### Signature feature
A private pool with open views down to the Córdoba valley — total privacy,
swimwear optional. Fully enclosed and fully private.

## 5. House rules & policies

- **Maximum guests:** 4
- **Minimum stay:** 3 nights
- **Children:** the property is not suitable for children (no childproofing,
  unfenced pool and drops on the hillside). If a guest asks about bringing
  kids, explain this politely and offer to have the host follow up if they
  have questions.
- **Pets:** allowed by arrangement only. If a guest asks about bringing a pet,
  ask for details (type/breed, size, number of pets) and let them know the
  host will confirm — encourage them to also message via WhatsApp or email so
  the host has the details directly.
- **Parties / events:** not allowed.
- **Smoking:** not allowed inside the house.
- **Quiet hours:** 10pm, per the neighborhood's rules (no loud music/noise
  after this time).
- **Check-in:** between 12pm (noon) and 7pm. Guests are encouraged to arrive
  while it's still light out — there are no streetlights in the area and the
  road can be tricky to find after dark.
- **Check-out:** 11am, but late check-out is often possible if there's no
  next booking — guests should just ask.
- **Early check-in:** may be possible depending on the schedule — guests
  should ask and the host will confirm.

## 6b. Deposits & cancellation policy

- **Booking deposit:** 50% of the total booking cost is required to reserve
  the dates.
- **Balance:** the remaining 50% is due no later than the day before
  check-in.
- **Damage/security deposit:** a separate $200 USD refundable damage deposit
  is also required (held against damage to the property; refunded after
  checkout if there's no damage).
- **Cancellation:**
  - Cancelled 2+ months before the stay: full refund (of the booking
    deposit/payments).
  - Cancelled 1–2 months before the stay: 50% refund (the deposit is
    forfeited).
  - Cancelled less than 1 month before the stay: no refund.
